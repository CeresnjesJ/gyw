Privacy Policy

Guide Your World  ·  Last updated: November 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Guide Your World ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and handles information when you use our content publishing application ("GYW Publishing") and visit our website.

1. Information We Collect

When you authorize GYW Publishing to connect to your social media accounts, we may access:

  • Basic account information (username, account ID) provided by the platform
  • Permissions required to publish content on your behalf

We do not collect personal data beyond what is necessary to perform the publishing function.

2. How We Use Information

We use the information solely to:

  • Publish content (images, videos, captions) to your authorized social media accounts
  • Manage scheduling and posting on your behalf

We do not sell, rent, or share your information with third parties for marketing purposes.

3. Data Storage

Access tokens required for publishing are stored securely and used only to authenticate API requests. We do not store your social media passwords.

4. Third-Party Platforms

GYW Publishing integrates with third-party platforms including TikTok, Instagram, Facebook, Pinterest, and YouTube. Use of these platforms is subject to their respective privacy policies and terms of service. We are not responsible for the data practices of these platforms.

5. Data Retention

We retain access tokens only as long as necessary to provide the publishing service. You may revoke access at any time through your social media account settings.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Revoke the application's access to your social media accounts at any time
  • Request deletion of any data we hold about you
  • Contact us with any privacy-related questions
